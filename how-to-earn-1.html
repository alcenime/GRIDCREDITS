HOW TO EARN $GRIDS

There are two ways to earn through the GridCredits ecosystem.

01

EARN

Own a GridCredits NFT and activate it through the EARN page.

Once activated, your character participates in the GridCredits earning system and generates $GRIDS according to its active earning rules.

OWN NFT→ACTIVATE→EARN $GRIDS
GO TO EARN ↗
02

BURN

You can also earn $GRIDS by permanently burning your GridCredits NFT.

Go to the BURN page, select the character you want to burn, review the $GRIDS burn value, and confirm the burn.

Once burned, the NFT is permanently removed from your collection and the corresponding $GRIDS is received according to the active burn rules.

SELECT NFT→BURN→RECEIVE $GRIDS
GO TO BURN ↗
